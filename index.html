The basics

What is PCOS?

Polycystic ovary syndrome is a common hormonal condition. Its presentation can look different from person to person.

01

Common signs

Irregular or absent periods, acne, excess facial or body hair, scalp hair thinning, weight changes, and difficulty becoming pregnant can be signs to discuss.

02

How it’s assessed

Clinicians look at your menstrual history, symptoms, blood work and sometimes an ultrasound, while ruling out other possible causes.

03

Care can help

Support may include lifestyle care, medication, and treatment tailored to your goals—whether that is cycle management, skin concerns, or fertility.
